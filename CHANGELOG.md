# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16): the release candidate (M12)

- The first full release for Poki: all twenty battles, the ending, Last Stand, eleven languages. Nothing in the game
  changed since 0.25.0; this build comes with its store page (thumbnails, a hover video, screenshots, the description
  in every language).

## 0.25.0 (2026-11-08): easier on everyone (M11, part 3)

- **Reduce motion:** a new switch in Settings (on already if your device asks for less motion). It calms the camera,
  shortens the army reveal and stops the screen's little animations.
- **Phones vibrate** for the big moments: a hit, a knockout, a boss changing or falling, a blast close by, a purchase.
  Turn it off in Settings.
- **Switching tabs pauses a wave** and silences the game; it waits for you when you come back.
- The Iron Ox's fire, a charge's warning lane and your health bar are brighter, so they're easier to see for
  colour-blind players.
- A proper credits screen.

## 0.24.0 (2026-11-07): eleven languages (M11, part 2)

- **Thunder Knight speaks your language:** French, Italian, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Turkish, Russian,
  Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean, as well as English. The game picks yours by itself, and you can change it
  in Pause, Settings: it switches at once.
- **Queen Ada on the map:** the battle card now has a line from the Queen about the fight ahead, and she cheers each
  region you free.
- The credits now name everyone whose models and sounds the game uses.

## 0.23.0 (2026-11-07): a faster first load (M11, part 1)

- **Less to download before you play:** the later regions' scenery, the riders' horses and the War Wagon's oxen now
  arrive in the background while you play the first battles. If you jump into a later battle before they're in, its
  button says "Loading…" for a moment.
- Two bits of scenery no battle used were left out of the download.

## 0.22.0 (2026-11-07): the ending (M10 complete)

- **The Iron Baron gets away... on a donkey.** When the Iron Ox falls, the Baron pops out of his turret, hops onto a
  little grey donkey and gallops off, shaking his fist and looking back at you. He does it every time you beat the
  Ox, in Last Stand too.
- **The ending:** win the final battle for the first time and Queen Ada walks up the hill to meet you at the banner.
  She and Rosie have a few words, everyone cheers, and the card reads "Larkmoor is free!" with Last Stand unlocked.
  Tap, click or press a key to move the lines on, or Skip.
- Replaying the final battle goes straight to the usual victory card.

## 0.21.0 (2026-11-07): Last Stand (M10, part 1)

- **Last Stand:** once you've beaten the Iron Baron, every battlefield you've cleared offers a Last Stand on its map
  card: endless waves that get bigger and tougher, for as long as your banner holds.
  - Rosie's truck opens after every third wave; between the others the next wave comes on its own after a short
    countdown.
  - A boss rides in every tenth wave: the War Wagon, the Siege Tower, the Iron Ox, then Sir Grudge.
  - Gold pays as usual, and each field keeps its best wave, shown on its map card. Beat it and the card says "New
    best!".
- Long battles no longer slowly pile up finished squads in the background.
- The Iron Ox calls two smaller blocks when it's wounded, one after the other: together they overran the banner while
  you fought the Ox.

## 0.20.0 (2026-11-07): guns show their stars (M9 complete)

- **Upgraded guns look upgraded:** at ★3 a gun gets a red-dot sight on top (the shotgun and the hunting rifle get a
  drum magazine instead), and at ★5 it gets gold bands and gold plates down its sides.

## 0.19.0 (2026-11-07): the Iron Baron (M9, part 9)

- **The final battle: The Iron Keep.** Six waves under the Keep's own wall, the last shop, then the Iron Baron himself
  in the Iron Ox, a bull-shaped iron engine with glowing eyes and a tiny turret where the Baron sits in his crown.
- **The Iron Ox breathes fire:** its horns glow and a cone lights up on the ground where the fire will go. Step out of
  it! The fire burns your soldiers, and the banner if the Ox is close enough.
- **It fights in three stages:** at first its armour shrugs off half of every bullet (explosions hit fully). Wounded,
  it calls two blocks led by elite captains. Badly hurt, its armour plates fall away and its glowing core shows: now
  rockets and grenades hit it even harder. Knock it out and the Baron's whole army runs.
- All twenty battles of the campaign can now be played. (The Baron's getaway, the ending and Last Stand come next.)

## 0.18.0 (2026-11-07): elite captains and rockets (M9, part 8)

- **Two more battles at the Iron Keep:** The Gatehouse and Inner Bailey, eight waves each. Inner Bailey throws
  everything the Baron has at you except the Baron himself.
- **Elite captains:** the Baron's household knights lead his blocks in gilded armour with a cape and a great sword.
  They take twice the beating of a captain, but knock one out and his whole block runs, not just the men near him.
- **The rocket launcher** goes on sale at The Gatehouse: three rockets that fly straight and fast, trailing smoke, and
  burst wide. They hit rams, catapults, siege towers and bosses twice as hard. Rosie's truck marks it "Save for this".
- The outer wall's gatehouse at The Gatehouse, and the Baron's yard (a well, tents, weapon racks) at Inner Bailey.

## 0.17.0 (2026-11-07): into the Iron Keep (M9, part 7)

- **The last region opens:** the Iron Keep, the Baron's own lands, starts with Burnt Orchard and Moat Road. Its soldiers
  are tougher again and drop more gold.
- **The Iron Keep looks like itself:** the Baron's castle stands on the skyline in the mist, his burnt orchards line
  the fields, and the grass is dry and trampled. Burnt Orchard has rows of charred trees and a burnt-out farmhouse;
  Moat Road runs past the castle's moat, with a bridge to a gate in its outer wall.
- **The minigun** goes on sale at Burnt Orchard: hold the trigger and its six barrels spin up, then it pours out 25
  rounds a second from a 200-round box. You walk a little slower while it fires. Rosie's truck marks it "Save for this".
- **Moat Road** throws rams and catapults at you together.

## 0.16.0 (2026-11-07): the Siege Tower (M9, part 6)

- **Pass of Echoes ends the Highland Pass:** six waves with everything the region has shown, the last shop, then the
  Baron's Siege Tower.
- **The Siege Tower:** a huge wooden tower pushed at your banner by ten soldiers. Bullets can't hurt the tower itself:
  - Knock out its pushers and it gets stuck where it stands ("Tower stuck! Hit the beams!").
  - Every so often its side doors swing open and a squad of footmen marches out.
  - Shoot the three glowing beams on its front. Break all three and it topples over, and the Baron's army runs.
  - Its beams show on the boss bar.
- The Baron's camp stands beyond the hedge at Pass of Echoes, with the pass's cliffs on either side.
- Big battles with rams, catapults and the tower run lighter on slower devices.

## 0.15.0 (2026-11-07): knights, a Sentry Gun and catapults (M9, part 5)

- **Two more battles in the Highland Pass:** Shepherd's Rest and Cliffside.
- **Hire the Queen's knights** in the Army tab from Shepherd's Rest: blue riders who wait beside the banner and charge
  into the Baron's men when they come close, knocking them flying, then fight on.
- **The Sentry Gun:** press E (or tap the support button) and Rosie drops a turret beside you. For 25 seconds it turns
  to the nearest enemy it can see and fires. Rosie lends you hers in Shepherd's Rest; after that it's in the Hero tab.
  If you own both call-ins, the one you're not using shows "Equip" in the shop, and the support button shows which one
  is ready.
- **Catapults:** three soldiers roll a catapult to the far side of the field, and it lobs boulders at your soldiers or
  the banner. Watch for the shadow growing on the ground and step out of it. Knock out its crew and it's left behind;
  grenades and the Rift Strike hurt the catapult itself.
- **The grenade launcher** goes on sale at Cliffside: six rounds that burst where they land, great on packed blocks
  and shield walls. Rosie's truck marks it "Save for this".
- A shepherd's cottage and fold at Shepherd's Rest, and a cliff with fallen boulders at Cliffside.
- Grenades break the battering ram sooner: it had kept its old, tougher health by mistake.
- The Highland Pass's soldiers are a little less tough, and Pine Gate a little smaller: it was harder than the battles
  after it.
- Fixed: after a ram or catapult fell, knocking out some soldiers could knock out others too, sometimes your own.

## 0.14.0 (2026-11-06): the Highland Pass opens (M9, part 4)

- **Two battles open the third region, the Highland Pass:** Pine Gate and Old Watchtower. Enemies there are tougher
  again and drop more gold.
- **The Brute:** a giant barbarian with a great axe and a health bar over his head. He stops and glows, the ground
  lights up where he'll run, then he charges down it, bowling over anyone in the way. Step aside. Sometimes two come.
- **The battering ram:** six soldiers push a covered ram at your banner. Bullets bounce off it, but its crew don't:
  each one you knock down slows it, and with none left it's abandoned. Grenades and the Rift Strike hurt the ram itself.
  If it reaches the banner, its log thumps into it.
- **The machine gun** goes on sale after Old Watchtower: a heavy gun with a drum magazine, 100 rounds and bullets that
  go through two soldiers. Rosie's truck marks it "Save for this".
- **Each region looks like itself:** the Riverlands have leafy round trees, and reeds and water lilies along their
  rivers; the Highland Pass has tall pines with mountains in the mist behind them, a stone gate at Pine Gate and an
  old tower at Old Watchtower.

## 0.13.0 (2026-11-06): the War Wagon (M9, part 3)

- **The Long Road ends the Riverlands:** five waves down an old road, the last shop, then the Baron's War Wagon.
- **The War Wagon:** an armoured wagon pulled by two oxen, with crossbowmen on its deck. Bullets bounce off its hull,
  so shoot its four wheels. Break two and it gets stuck: its crossbows shoot faster and footmen pour out of the back.
  Break all four and it collapses, and the Baron's army runs. Its wheels show on the boss bar.
- **The hunting rifle** opens at The Long Road, and Rosie's truck marks it "Save for this".
- A dirt road with fences and milestones runs down the middle of The Long Road.

## 0.12.0 (2026-11-05): heavier steel (M9, part 2)

- **Two more battles in the Riverlands:** The Ferry and Willow Palisade.
- **Rift Strike:** press E (or tap the new button) and Rosie drops six shells through the rift onto the biggest
  crowd near your aim. A red circle warns first. She gives you one free in The Ferry; after that it's in the Hero tab,
  with more strikes a wave as you upgrade it.
- **Men-at-arms:** the Baron's knights in full plate with great swords. Pistols and SMGs only do half against them,
  so bring a rifle or the shotgun. Rosie warns you the first time. You can hire your own men-at-arms in the Army tab
  from Willow Palisade; they hold a line in front of the banner.
- **New guns:** the assault rifle (from The Ferry), a strong all-rounder that shoots through two soldiers, and the
  hunting rifle (from The Long Road, coming next). Each has its own look in your hand, its own sound and a picture in
  the shop.
- Rosie's truck marks the assault rifle "Save for this" in The Ferry and Willow Palisade.
- A ferry landing with a log raft at The Ferry, and a stake wall round the willows at Willow Palisade.

## 0.11.0 (2026-11-05): into the Riverlands (M9, part 1)

- **Two new battles open the second region:** Ford of Reeds and Marsh Village. Enemies there are tougher and drop
  more gold.
- **Riders:** horsemen trot in, then charge you or the banner, knocking your soldiers aside. A shotgun hit knocks a
  rider off his horse, and the horse runs off. Listen for the hoofbeats.
- **Firebrands:** men with lit fire-pots over their heads sprint at you or the banner and burst, hurting everyone
  near, their own side too. Shoot them early, while they're among their friends.
- **Two guns:** from Battle 6 you carry two guns. Swap with X, 1, 2 or the mouse wheel, or tap the gun button.
  Rosie shows you once.
- Rosie's truck marks the SMG "Save for this" in Battles 6 and 7.
- A river with stepping stones and reeds at Ford of Reeds; cottages, a well and a muddy marsh at Marsh Village.

## 0.10.0 (2026-11-05): the battle has a voice (M8)

- **Real sounds:** each gun has its own shot, bullets tick on cloth and clink on armour, and knocked-out soldiers yelp
  as they flop into the grass.
- **The battle's beats:** the Baron's horn as each wave comes, war drums under the army's arrival, Larkmoor's horn
  when a captain's men run, a sting when a wave is cleared, a warning drum when they reach the banner, and a fanfare
  for a win.
- **Music:** a light orchestral tune between waves and on the map, a march in waves, and its own music for Sir
  Grudge. Turn it down in Settings (Music).
- Shield Walls clonk when your bullets hit their shields, crack, and splinter under a grenade. Archers' bows go
  "thwip". Sir Grudge roars before each charge and lands hard when he falls.
- Gold pickups tick a little higher with each coin in a row.
- **Hits feel bigger:** knocked-out soldiers slide back a little, and the battle pauses for a blink when a captain's
  men run or Sir Grudge falls.
- What you buy at Rosie's truck now drops out of the sky in a crate beside her.
- A soft countryside sound plays under the battle and dips for a moment when you fire.

## 0.7.0 (2026-11-05): Larkmoor's last stand (M6)

- **Two new battles finish the first region:** Hollow Farm and Grudge's Hill.
- **Shield Walls:** soldiers behind tall shields that stop your bullets from the front. Get round to their side, throw
  a grenade (it smashes the shield), or let your militia hack at them. Shields crack before they break.
- **Sir Grudge, the Black Champion,** a giant knight, ends the region:
  - He marches on the banner behind a shield that won't break: shoot him from the side.
  - Hurt him enough and he throws the shield down and charges. Watch for the red glow on the ground and step aside.
  - Knock him out and his whole army runs.
  - His name and health bar show at the top while he's on the field, and Rosie warns you at the last shop before him.
- **The shotgun knocks soldiers back,** and its upgrades are cheaper to start.
- **Rosie's truck** marks the shotgun "Save for this" in Battles 4 and 5, so you know what to save for.
- If you lose a battle twice in a row, Rosie now tells you she's made your soldiers a little tougher.
- The map shows little landmarks beside the first five battles.

## 0.6.0 (2026-11-05): optional ads for a boost (M5)

- **Double your gold:** after a win you can watch a short video to get your knockout gold and wave bonuses again.
- **Rally:** if the banner falls, watch a video to rally once per battle. The banner flares back to half strength, the
  enemies around it are knocked flat, and you carry on with the same wave.
- **Rift crates:** from Battle 4, a crate sometimes falls beside the truck between waves. Walk up to it to borrow a
  powerful gun you don't own yet for one wave, by watching a video, or say No thanks.
- These are always your choice: "Continue", "Retry" and "No thanks" come first. If a video doesn't play, nothing is
  lost and the game carries on.
- **Short ads** may play between waves (when you press Next wave) and after a result card, from Battle 2 on. Never in
  Battle 1, never in the middle of a fight, and not right after a video you chose.
- Fixed: the game no longer asks for a sound file that doesn't exist each time it starts.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-05): learning by playing (M4)

- **Battle 1 teaches you as you go,** one thing at a time: walk to the glowing ring, aim at the scout, shoot, collect
  his gold, hold off the mob, visit Rosie's truck, buy, start the next wave, then knock out the captain.
  - Pictures of the keys and mouse on a computer, an animated hand on a phone, and never more than four words.
  - Stuck? The hint pulses, Rosie gives a tip, and glowing footprints show the way.
  - The screen starts almost empty; gold, the bars and your health appear when you first need them.
- **Later battles teach one new thing each:** grenades in Battle 2, hiring help at the truck, and hero gear in Battle 3.
  Switch from a computer to a phone and you're shown the touch controls once.
- **Rosie talks to you** in small bubbles at the edge of the screen.
- **A look at each wave:** the camera rises behind the banner as the enemy marches in. New enemy types get a name and
  a tip ("Captain: they'll run!"). Press anything to skip it, or set Wave intros to Short or Off.
- **Arrows at the screen's edge** point to the banner when it's under attack, to new squads arriving, and to the truck
  between waves.
- **Settings** in Pause: sound, music, look speed, invert look, auto-fire, aim help, wave intros, screen shake,
  graphics, HUD size, left-handed controls and vibration on phones, and credits.
- **Back to map** in Pause now asks first, and reminds you your gold is kept.
- **Fits every screen,** from small phones to 4K, upright or sideways:
  - The top bars step aside when your gold grows long.
  - The truck's panel fits small phones with Next wave beside it.
  - On a small screen the map zooms to where you are, so every battle is easy to tap.
  - Nothing sits under Poki's corner button.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-04): the battle loop (M3)

- **Three battles to play:** The Mill, Turnip Field and Old Stone Bridge.
  - New players start straight in the first one.
  - Returning players start on the map of Larkmoor, which shows all 20 battles; the later ones are locked for now.
- **Defend the Queen's banner.** Enemies march into the ring round it and wear it down; knock them out to stop it.
  The banner mends a little between waves.
- **Gold:** every knocked-out enemy drops coins. They fly to you when you're close, and every coin left flies to you
  when a wave ends. Waves and first wins pay bonuses. Gold is never lost.
- **Rosie's truck:** between waves, walk into the gold ring by the truck to shop.
  - Guns, Army and Hero tabs open over the first three battles.
  - A "Best buy" ribbon suggests what to get.
- **Next wave** starts when you're ready: there's no timer.
- **Captains:** knock one out and his squad runs off, dropping their gold.
- **Grenades** from Battle 2: G or the grenade button. They land on the thickest bunch of enemies near your aim.
- **You can be knocked out** (not in Battle 1). The world goes grey for five seconds, then you're back up at the
  banner.
- **Wins and losses:**
  - Victory: up to three crowns and the gold you earned.
  - If the banner falls, you keep your gold and can retry or go to the map.
  - Pause has "Back to map".
- **Progress saves:** gold, guns, upgrades and battles won.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-04): the armies (M2)

- **Two armies fight on the field:**
  - The Baron's footmen, captains, archers and peasants march on the Queen's banner.
  - Her militia, men-at-arms and archers hold a line in front of it.
  - Up to 300 soldiers at once on strong devices, 160 on phones.
- **Soldiers fight each other on their own:**
  - They march in formation, break ranks to fight, and take a moment to recover from a hit.
  - When knocked out they tumble away from the blow and sink into the grass. No blood.
- **Captains carry tall red banners.** Knock one out and his squad runs away.
- **Archers loose bolts in arcs.** A red ring on the ground shows where the Baron's bolts will land.
- **Soldiers who reach you swing at you.** A red arc shows which side the hit came from.
- **Easy to tell apart:** the Baron's knights wear dark steel and red; the Queen's wear bright steel and blue.
- **The Baron's reserves** march along the far hills.
- Characters are from the free KayKit Adventurers pack by Kay Lousberg, for now; the art pass may change them.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-04): camera and gun feel (M1)

- **Shooting:** the pistol fires while you hold the mouse button. On touch it fires by itself when the crosshair is on
  a soldier; a small dot under the crosshair shows when.
- **Reloading:** automatic when the magazine is empty or after a short pause. Press R, or tap the gun button, to reload
  early.
- **Every hit shows:** the soldier flashes, with a puff of dust and a small number. A tick on the crosshair confirms
  it, and the tick turns gold when the soldier goes down.
- **Knockouts:** soldiers tumble over and sink into the grass. No blood, ever.
- **Aim help:**
  - Your look slows a little over a soldier, and shots lean toward the one you're aiming at.
  - On touch the view drifts gently toward targets.
  - Captains (the ones with tall red banners) get a gold diamond on the crosshair.
- **The camera** sits higher and further back, so you see more of the field and less of the hero. It no longer goes
  through Rosie's truck, and the hero turns see-through when the camera is pushed close.
- **The banner hill** is higher, so you look down on the army marching up to it.
- **A practice range for testing:**
  - soldiers standing at set distances
  - a line of peasants walking back and forth
  - a block of 60 soldiers with a captain marching up the hill
- Placeholder gun sounds until the sound pass.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04): foundations (M0)

- The game boots straight onto a greybox meadow: the Queen's banner on its hill, Rosie's truck, and the hero, seen over
  the shoulder.
- **Walking:** with WASD or arrows on a computer (the first click captures the mouse for looking), and with a floating
  stick on touch screens.
- **Pausing:** Esc, P or the pause button; Resume continues.
- Works in landscape and portrait.
- Poki's SDK is wired: loading, gameplay start and stop. No ads yet; they come with the battle loop.
- Progress and settings save safely, even where storage is blocked.
